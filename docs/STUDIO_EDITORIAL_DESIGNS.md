# Brand Studio editorial collection

Nine export-safe compositions extend the existing renderer: Career Ledger, Signal and Ticket; Scholarship Atlas, Journal and Fellow; Deadline Clock, Bulletin and Calendar. Choose designs in the Studio gallery or existing selector. Existing saved designs remain editable and take priority over automatic choices.

New automatic opportunity batches rotate by category and Afghanistan calendar date. Successive unsaved posts in each category cycle through all three designs. Selection is deterministic for retries. Deadline reminders have their own collection regardless of the underlying opportunity category. Other editorial formats retain their existing renderers. No publishing permissions, delivery destinations or worker schedules are changed.

Deadline previews refresh every 30 seconds. They use the source deadline, show hours near closing, never count below zero, and identify missing/invalid dates. Date-only editor values mean the end of that date in Afghanistan; source timestamps keep their explicit instant. PNGs are static, so every deadline export includes its snapshot time and official date. Images already posted to channels cannot become ticking clocks.

All new poster labels support English, Dari and Pashto. Listing text is preserved from the source. Layouts support 1080×1080, 1080×1350 and 1080×1920 with reserved QR/footer space. The CI visual suite exports all nine designs in all languages and sizes using public listing snapshots, blocks external browser requests and verifies title retention, content fit, footer clearance and dimensions. No posts are sent by the review.

The ARI mascot candidate remains on its separate, unmerged branch. This main-branch redesign does not imply approval or deployment of that candidate.
