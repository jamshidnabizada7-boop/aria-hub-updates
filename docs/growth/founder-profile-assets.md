# Day 7 — Founder profile assets (Ahmad Jamshid, Founder — ARIA HUB)

## 100-word bio (LinkedIn About / directories)

> I am Ahmad Jamshid, founder of ARIA HUB (myariahub.com), a multilingual platform that helps people from Afghanistan find jobs, scholarships and global opportunities with clear summaries and direct official application routes. ARIA HUB reviews, summarizes and publishes listings in English, Dari and Pashto, updated daily, and gives employers and organizations a free way to reach Afghan applicants. I built it after seeing how much talent in Afghanistan is lost to scattered, outdated and misleading information. My focus: trustworthy opportunity access, transparent operations and partnerships that help students and professionals act before deadlines pass.

## 300-word story (founder page / press use)

> Growing up around Afghan students and job seekers, I kept seeing the same problem: opportunities existed, but the information around them did not. Deadlines were buried in foreign-language pages, application links were misleading or dead, and the same questions were answered from scratch in every group chat, every semester.
>
> ARIA HUB started as an answer to that. It collects current jobs, scholarships, internships, fellowships and training programs into one searchable platform. Every listing is reviewed by our editorial workflow, summarized clearly, translated into Dari (Persian) and Pashto as well as English, and — most importantly — linked to the official application route, so applicants act on the real source instead of a copy.
>
> The platform updates daily. Employers and organizations can submit opportunities for free during our launch phase, and we translate and distribute them across our channels. Tools like the CV Builder, deadline calendar and Opportunity Matcher exist for one reason: to move a person from "I heard about something" to a submitted application before the deadline.
>
> What I care about most is trust. We do not route people through aggregators when a direct official form exists, we do not guarantee admission or jobs, and we treat numbers with evidence rather than enthusiasm. ARIA HUB is still early, and I share what is being improved openly — because credibility with students, parents, employers and partners is the only asset that compounds.
>
> If you run an education or youth organization, a campus group or a media desk, I would like to hear from you: info@myariahub.com.
