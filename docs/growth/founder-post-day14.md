## What ARIA HUB solves — and how we check every application link

**By Ahmad Jamshid, Founder — ARIA HUB**

Every semester, the same thing happens to talented people across Afghanistan:
an opportunity appears in a group chat — a scholarship, a job, a fellowship —
and nobody can tell whether the link behind it is real, current, or official.
Some links are copies of copies. Some are outdated. A few are outright
misleading. Talented applicants lose real chances not because they weren't good
enough, but because the path to the application was broken.

ARIA HUB exists to fix that specific problem. We collect current jobs,
scholarships, internships, fellowships and training programs, summarize them
clearly, translate them into Dari (Persian) and Pashto as well as English, and
update the catalog daily.

**How official apply links are checked.** Our editorial workflow reviews each
listing before publication and links to the official application route — the
employer's own form, the university's own portal, the program's own email. When
a direct official form exists, we do not route applicants through an aggregator
page. When a listing's deadline passes, it leaves the active listings; expired
programs don't pretend to be open.

**What we are being honest about.** ARIA HUB is still early and improving. We
do not guarantee admission, visas or jobs — nobody honestly can. We don't quote
audience numbers without dated evidence, and we're building our success-story
section to separate what users submitted from what we verified.

**An invitation.** If you run an education or youth organization, a campus
group, or a media desk, we built something for you: a free, embeddable ARIA HUB
opportunity feed with tracked links, and a simple partnership model — co-posts,
co-hosted webinars, or a resource-page link. See myariahub.com/partners, or
write to info@myariahub.com. If you simply want never to miss a deadline:
myariahub.com/opportunities.
